AAOSMusic for Apple Music: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

AAOSMusic for Apple Music ("the app") is an independent player that lets Apple Music subscribers play their own Apple Music library in cars running Android Automotive OS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc. Apple and Apple Music are trademarks of Apple Inc.

What the app handles

  • Apple Music access token. When you sign in, you approve access on your phone through Apple's own sign-in page. Your Apple ID password goes only to Apple. The resulting Apple Music user token is passed to the car through our sign-in relay and stored on the car in the app's private storage, so you don't have to sign in every drive. Signing out deletes it from the car.
  • Sign-in relay. During sign-in, the relay (hosted on Supabase) briefly holds a one-time code and the token so the car can collect it. That record is deleted when the car collects the token, or after 5 minutes. To limit abuse, the relay stores a salted hash of the requesting IP address alongside each sign-in attempt, never the address itself.
  • Music data. The app requests your library, playlists, recently played items, recommendations and search results directly from Apple's Apple Music API in order to display and play them. It doesn't send this data anywhere else. Playback is reported to Apple as with any Apple Music app.
  • Debug reports (optional). Only if you tap Send debug report, the app uploads a diagnostic report: app version, device model and Android version, screen size, whether you are signed in (yes/no), the app's recent internal log (which may include song titles and error messages) and the app's own system log. Access tokens are removed before upload. Reports are used only to fix problems.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties, apart from the service providers needed to run the app: Apple (music service) and Supabase (sign-in relay, debug reports).

Retention and deletion

Sign-in relay records: up to 5 minutes. Debug reports: kept until the issue is resolved, then deleted. To delete the data on the car, sign out or uninstall the app. To have debug reports deleted, contact the developer using the email address on the Google Play listing.
